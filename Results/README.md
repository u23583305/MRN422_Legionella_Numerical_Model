
# Numerical Simulation Results

This folder contains the CSV results generated by the numerical model developed for the MRN 422 research project.

## 24-Hour Simulation Results

- `24h_no_recirc.csv` – Temperature results without recirculation.
- `24h_uninsulated_recirc.csv` – Temperature results with uninsulated recirculation.
- `24h_insulated_recirc.csv` – Temperature results with 10 mm pipe insulation and recirculation.
- `Table_10_24h_Risk_Comparison.csv` – Comparison of thermal performance and pump operation.
- `Table_11_48C_Thermal_Performance.csv` – Thermal performance relative to the 48 °C threshold.

## Recirculation Optimisation Results

- `Optimisation_1_to_10_min_results.csv` – Results for recirculation durations from 1 to 10 minutes under insulated and uninsulated conditions.

The optimisation results include the final temperature at thermocouple 9A, thermal-risk exposure, cycle duration and pump duty cycle.

The CSV files allow the numerical results to be inspected without rerunning the simulations.
